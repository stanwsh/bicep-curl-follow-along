Preparation

  1. Sit upright with the working upper arm stationary beside the torso.
  2. Keep the forearm supinated (palm up) and unsupported during holds and curls.
  3. Begin at 90° elbow flexion and curl toward approximately 135°–145°.
  4. Practice the slow movement, then the fast movement shown on screen.
  5. During supported relaxation, place the forearm on the thigh or chair support and release the dumbbell.
